## The bootstrap #94 cannot self-provide

#96 contains an exact-head CI dispatcher, and that is the right design. It
cannot unblock #96 itself:

- the worker's connector-driven pushes and PR reopens do not schedule
  `pull_request` workflow runs — PR #96 carried four commits across two heads
  with zero check runs, confirmed on `3e9f5bcd` and `8a77acf9`;
- the dispatcher that would fix this runs from `aren-train-controller.yml`,
  which only executes once it is on the default branch;
- so #96 needs green exact-head CI to merge, and needs to be merged to produce
  green exact-head CI.

`/agent-ci <branch>` breaks that cycle from outside: it lives on `main`, is
triggered by a comment the worker can write, and dispatches `aren-ci` at the
branch head. See [`ops/agent-exec.md`](../agent-exec.md).

**Use it on #96 now.** Post `/agent-ci chat1/94-autonomous-train-controller`,
then verify the check runs against the exact head SHA you intend to merge, per
§4 of [`ops/control-plane.md`](../control-plane.md).

## Standing correction

A head with no workflow run is not `CI pending`. It is a trigger failure, and
it is actionable: dispatch the run. CONTROL already forbids reporting it as
passive waiting — this is the mechanism that makes that instruction executable.

Once #96 is merged, its controller owns the dispatch and `/agent-ci` becomes
the manual fallback rather than the primary route.
